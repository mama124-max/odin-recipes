Description

This is a simple and delicious burger recipe that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Burger buns
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into patties.
  3. Grill the patties for 4-5 minutes per side.
  4. Toast the buns on the grill.
  5. Assemble the burger with your favorite toppings.
