Lasagna

Description

This is the ultimate lasagna recipe with a rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta cheese filling, and layers of melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles until al dente.
  3. Layer the meat sauce, noodles, and cheese in a baking dish.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
