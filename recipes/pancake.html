Description

This is a simple and delicious pancake recipe that's perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the wet ingredients.
  3. Combine the dry and wet ingredients.
  4. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour the batter onto the skillet.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden brown.
